# MFI Organization Model

## Purpose

The MFI Business Hub represents the operating structure of Multifaceted Investments LLC, a veteran-owned holding company. This document defines stable organizational concepts without treating planned AI agents as employees or independent executives.

## Business pillars

MFI organizes business ownership and long-term strategy around four pillars:

1. **Infrastructure & Supply Chain** — physical infrastructure, sourcing, logistics, manufacturing, trades, and related services.
2. **Technology / Gaming / Entertainment** — software, interactive media, gaming, entertainment, platforms, and adjacent intellectual property.
3. **Investments** — investment research, portfolio activities, acquisitions, capital allocation, and financial opportunities.
4. **Incubation** — formation, validation, and development of new ventures until they are assigned, spun out, or retired.

Government Business and Private Sector Business are cross-pillar revenue functions, not additional pillars. They coordinate market channels and opportunities that may involve any pillar.

## Seat types

Every organizational seat has one of four types:

- `HUMAN`: held and performed by a person.
- `AI`: assigned to a registered AI agent within explicit authority.
- `HYBRID`: a human-owned role supported by one or more registered agents.
- `VACANT`: not currently assigned.

A human always outranks an AI agent in the same functional area. A vacant human seat does not transfer executive, approval, contractual, financial, legal, or employment authority to an AI agent.

## Roles and agents

An organizational role describes accountability. An agent is a governed software identity that may support one or more roles. A model provider is an execution dependency, not a role or agent identity.

```text
Organizational accountability -> Registered agent -> Model Gateway -> Provider
```

Changing from a local model to Claude, OpenAI, Grok, or another approved provider does not change the agent's name, responsibilities, or authority.

## Leadership and escalation

Pillar leads coordinate work within their pillars. Cross-company leads coordinate shared revenue or operating functions. Executive copilots advise their corresponding human executives; they do not become corporate officers or acquire executive approval power.

When ownership overlaps, work escalates to the accountable human or designated hybrid seat. The [Authority Matrix](AUTHORITY_MATRIX.md) governs what may be read, written, proposed, approved, and executed.
